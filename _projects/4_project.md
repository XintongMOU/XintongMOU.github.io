**Person-in-Charge & Co-Founder** · Incubated by HK Tech 300 & HKSTP Ideation · Mar–Sep 2025

DecentraScholars proposed a novel **Decentralized Science (DeSci)** infrastructure — the "Web3 Brain of Science" — to automate and decentralize academic research workflows.

**Funding & Recognition:**
- Secured a competitive **seed grant from HK Tech 300** (SF202503165), ranking among top-tier applicants
- Selected for **HKSTP Ideation** programme incubation

**Technical Architecture:**
- Deployed a **Multi-Agent System (MAS)** where specialized AI agents automate research workflows end-to-end
- Integrated **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** and **Citation Knowledge Graphs** for semantic reasoning across literature
- Engineered a scalable trusted data layer using **Solana** (high-throughput attribution) and **Arweave** (immutable storage) to solve the scientific reproducibility crisis

**Outcome:** The venture was voluntarily concluded prior to incorporation to comply with visa and regulatory requirements, enabling a strategic pivot to quantitative finance research.
