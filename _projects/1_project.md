**Research Leader** · City University of Hong Kong · Advisor: Prof. LUO Rui · Jun 2025–Present

This project investigates the causal effect of board composition and executive networks on corporate ESG disclosure using a panel dataset of over one million corporate records sourced from major financial databases.

**Methodology highlights:**

- Implemented the **Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) CSDID estimator** in R to address negative-weighting biases in staggered difference-in-differences designs
- Integrated **Social Network Analysis (SNA)** to decouple transmission channels from firm-level heterogeneity, identifying the structural network constraints that govern executive influence
- Conducted **Oster's (2019) Delta** sensitivity analyses and **500-round placebo tests** to validate treatment-effect non-randomness
- Engineered a full data pipeline in Python (Pandas, Statsmodels) to clean, merge, and structure the complex panel dataset

**Status:** Manuscript in preparation.
